PRIORITY CLIENT REPORTING
Three screens
Dashboard
The wall display. Four client panels, campaign and date filters, drill-down, auto-cycling when idle.
Nightly ingestion
Drop in Campaign Summary, Appointment Report, Agent Summary and Call Details. Feeds the dashboard directly.
Call Details aggregator
For when the contact definition needs tuning per client. Turns a 64MB export into a small hourly file.
Ingested data lives in the browser you ingest it in. To move a day's numbers to another machine or to the wall display, export the CSVs from the ingestion screen and commit them to data/.
